## Executive summary

ThalosPrime is an AI research and engineering software project intended to improve how complex problems are investigated, solutions are generated, and proposed implementations are tested. The system is being developed to coordinate AI models and specialized software components through a repeatable workflow that compares candidate approaches, checks for contradictions and implementation errors, and evaluates results against explicit criteria.

The immediate financing case should be presented as a defined software-development and validation plan, not as a claim that the project can solve arbitrary problems or that future commercial success is assured. The current repository contains software components, but the complete product's end-to-end effectiveness and commercial demand must be established through documented testing and customer validation.

## Milestone-based controls

Divide the project into stages. Release or commit spending against specific deliverables, such as:

1. Scope, requirements, budget, and baseline task set approved.
2. Core workflow integrated and runnable from a recorded version.
3. Tests and benchmark results produced, including failed cases and known limitations.
4. Independent or external review completed where feasible.
5. Pilot or customer-discovery results documented before expanding the budget.

For each milestone, record the owner, due date, budget, acceptance criteria, and evidence required to mark it complete.

## Risk controls

- Keep development and operating budgets separate from personal spending.
- Track spending by category and preserve receipts and invoices.
- Use written acceptance criteria for paid work and major purchases.
- Maintain a record of repository versions, test results, defects, and remediation.
- Avoid scaling compute or infrastructure costs before measuring demand and unit costs.
- Report actual progress against milestones, not just planned activity.
- Keep assumptions separate from verified revenue, contracts, assets, and other financial facts.

## Evidence to attach

Use only current, verifiable records:

- Repository URL and the exact commit or release being described.
- Recent test run and benchmark output, including the command, environment, date, and failures.
- Itemized budget and supporting vendor estimates.
- Financial statements and documents supporting the repayment source.
- Signed contracts, purchase orders, or pilot agreements, if they exist and may be disclosed.
- A risk register and milestone schedule.
