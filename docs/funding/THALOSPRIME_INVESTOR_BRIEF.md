# ThalosPrime — Investor Brief

**Status:** Draft. Add market, traction, team, financial, and financing facts only when supported by evidence.

## Executive summary

ThalosPrime is an AI research and engineering project intended to improve how complex problems are investigated, solutions are generated, and proposed implementations are tested. The proposed system will coordinate AI models and specialized software components to gather evidence, compare competing approaches, identify contradictions and implementation errors, and evaluate results against explicit criteria.

The product hypothesis is that users need more than generated answers: they need a repeatable way to determine which proposed solution works, under what conditions, at what cost, and with what limitations. ThalosPrime aims to provide that workflow. Its commercial value remains to be established through comparative testing and customer validation.

## Product strategy

The canonical repository will become the integration core. Other Thalos Prime repositories are candidate sources for specific capabilities, not separate features to be advertised wholesale. Components will be selected based on code quality, license compatibility, measurable utility, integration cost, security, and test evidence.

Initial focus should be repository engineering and other task classes where outcomes can be checked objectively. Expansion into additional domains should follow only after domain-specific validation.

## Potential differentiation

The potential advantage is a traceable loop combining task constraints, evidence provenance, candidate generation, representation and constraint checks, executable validation, baseline comparison, and recorded failure analysis. Multi-model coordination alone is not a moat. The differentiator must be demonstrated by better outcomes, lower total cost, greater traceability, or reduced risk on defined workloads.

## Validation gates

1. **Inventory:** understand the actual code, licenses, dependencies, and test status of candidate repositories.
2. **Integrated workflow:** demonstrate an end-to-end run with recorded inputs and outputs.
3. **Comparative advantage:** measure against a simple baseline on a predefined task set.
4. **Robustness:** report regressions, false positives, missed errors, repeatability, and cost.
5. **User validation:** document real workflows, user outcomes, willingness to pay, and continued use.
6. **Commercial validation:** establish pricing, acquisition channels, support costs, and unit economics.

## Current claims discipline

Repository READMEs describe intended capabilities, not necessarily independently verified implementation. No claim of product-market fit, recurring revenue, customer traction, defensible moat, patent coverage, or general technical superiority should be made without corresponding evidence. Distinguish current implementation from planned integration and experimental hypotheses.

## Investor-facing paragraph

ThalosPrime is being developed as an evidence-oriented AI research and engineering workflow. It aims to help users compare candidate solutions, detect errors and contradictions, and test results against explicit criteria rather than relying on a single generated answer. The opportunity depends on proving that this workflow improves outcomes on a defined initial class of tasks and delivers that improvement at a viable cost. The immediate plan is to audit the existing codebase, integrate only the strongest tested components, and establish comparative technical and customer evidence before scaling.
